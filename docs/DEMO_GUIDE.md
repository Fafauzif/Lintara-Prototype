# A short judge and tenant demonstration

## The story

“Lintara turns station movement into decisions a business can test. Watch an arrival, see how people reach commercial spaces, then compare a location and service plan with costs included. Operators see the whole plan; a tenant sees their own business case.”

## Ninety-second route

1. **Movement, 20 seconds.** Start in Station. Point to Quality next to the roles and the labeled Next train button. Click Next train: it seeks to the approach and plays without adding passengers. Switch Evening rush, then Start hour 18:00 to illustrate the authored commute-home model. Explain that these are planning assumptions that can be calibrated with station counts.
2. **Opportunity, 40 seconds.** Choose Business opportunity. Read the exact modeled sales, contribution after costs, break-even and service capacity for one business. Show the basket/margin/wage/purchase assumptions and Cautious/Base/Upside inputs. Click Find an opportunity; inspect its tested legal unit/staff combinations, then Apply the recommendation. If no positive result is found, that is useful evidence to reconsider the concept or inputs.
3. **Impact, 30 seconds.** Open Scenarios. Explain the baseline reference and readable monetary/service changes. A negative sign means the modeled value fell; whether this helps depends on the metric. Keep unfavorable financial outcomes visible and use them to improve the plan. Finish with the downloadable tenant opportunity brief.

## Reproducible positive example

Reset the plan to the default Normal crowd, seed 20261012, 07:00–08:00. Open Kopi Sela (T2, U2). Changing only staff from 2 to 3 produces these selected-hour authored model outcomes:

| Metric | 2 workers | 3 workers |
|---|---:|---:|
| Transactions | 82 | 127 |
| Sales | IDR 2,316,200 | IDR 3,565,100 |
| Labor | IDR 52,000 | IDR 78,000 |
| Contribution after modeled costs | IDR 1,452,525 | IDR 2,250,799 |
| Mean wait | 2.58 min | 2.07 min |
| Abandoned visits | 66 | 38 |

Contribution improves by IDR 798,274 for this business after the extra wage is included. This is a reproducible model example, not observed revenue or a guarantee for every tenant/window. The finder may recommend another tested unit/staff combination with a higher contribution. Other shop results can change through competition.

## Tenant conversation

“The value is seeing the inputs behind the projection. Enter your planned basket and margin, wage quotation and occupancy cost. Test a cautious and an upside case, check what sales must cover, and find a service/location option. A next pilot would calibrate these assumptions with counts, service observations and cost quotations.”

Contribution is operating gross margin after modeled labor, occupancy and campaigns, not full company net profit. One rush hour is not a whole trading day. The tenant report contains only the selected business's individual finances; role switching is a prototype workflow, not production authentication.

## Rehearsal

Install from the ZIP and run the browser test plan on the actual demonstration device. Verify gestures, focus, layout, train preview, quality switching, report download/print and FPS there. The delivered checks do not establish those browser/GPU properties.
